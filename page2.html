Design of Classrooms



Come and Discover how our classrooms, artistically enhanced by the design team of NAZARENO/LICHAUCO, support the philosophy and education method of Dr. Maria Montessori.



Each classroom has materials and spaces that encourage independent learning, collaboration, creativity, and hands-on activities to foster an environment where students can thrive.




ACADEMIC TRACKS


Seipsum Facit Persona



Casa


  • Pre-Kindergarten to Kindergarten
  • Ages 3-5
  • The Child forms and develops their intelligence by using objects in the environment.





Primary


  • Grades 1-3
  • Ages 6-8
  • The classroom's prepared environment allows the child to learn and grow at their own pace.





Intermediate


  • Grades 4-6
  • Ages 9-12
  • The child continues to develop their cognitive and social skills through teamwork and collaboration.





Junior High School


  • Grades 7-10
  • Ages 13-16
  • The Child strengthens their critical thinking skills to prepare for adult responsibilities.





Senior High School


  • Grades 11-12
  • Ages 17-18
  • The student is now preparing to enter tertiary level of any college. The curriculum is GAS plus.
