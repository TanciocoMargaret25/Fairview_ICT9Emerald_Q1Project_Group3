Welcome to OBMC Philippines!


At Operation Brother Hood Montessori, each child is nurtured and cared for in the vision of Maria Montessori for a better future.



Aas a progressive. Co-educational school offering Pre-k to grade 12, we are are an enriching alternative option compared to international schools.

While we envision the future of the children, we follow the montessori way of learning. Children who come to our school will follow the wise words—“Seipsum Facit Persona”, Man Makes Himself.

Our goal is to transform our students into self-productive, hard working, members of society who will lead, develop and shape and ever changing world.

Explore our programs and discover a vibrant community of people that makes OBMC an amazing place for a child to develop.
